# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AetherWall welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Participation in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions
requires respectful behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; critique the work, not the person.
- Be considerate of language differences, accessibility needs, and time constraints.
- Accept corrections, respect boundaries, and help others participate.
- Credit authors and obtain permission before sharing their content.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexualized conduct, impersonation,
spamming, and disclosure of private information without consent are unacceptable.
Do not retaliate against people who raise concerns in good faith.

## Reporting and moderation

For an ordinary discussion problem, mention the maintainer
[@Ordo-xsenos](https://github.com/Ordo-xsenos) in the relevant thread without
repeating abusive content or exposing personal details. Do not post confidential
allegations publicly. For harassment, threats, or private-information exposure,
use GitHub's [Report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/github-community-guidelines)
mechanism; it is handled privately by GitHub, not a confidential inbox for the maintainer.
GitHub's blocking and reporting tools remain available independently of this project.

Maintainers may remove content, ask for corrections, limit participation, or block
accounts according to the circumstances and GitHub policies. Reports are assessed
in context; these rules apply to maintainers as well as contributors. Project
moderation does not replace GitHub's enforcement process.
